# Lab 4 — Discovery and Responsible Outreach
**Student:** Ahmed Farah | **Team:** Aster
**Status:** Classroom planning exercise; all interviews and personas below are simulated, not real stakeholder statements.

## Value proposition
Our neighborhood listing platform helps property professionals create listings and helps local businesses submit sponsor messages. A simple approval system would make it easier to review information before it appears publicly. Accessible pages help visitors find useful information.

## Facts and assumptions
**Known from the class project:** the platform covers property listings and local sponsors; we need testable requirements and responsible outreach.
**Assumptions to verify:** professionals want status updates; sponsors want previews; users want simple forms; reviewers need a consistent approval process.

## Neutral interview questions
1. How do you currently create or update a property listing?
2. What information needs to be checked before a listing goes live?
3. What steps do you use to work with local businesses?
4. Who makes the final decision before content is published?
5. What information do visitors ask about most often?
6. How do you know whether a listing or sponsor placement is useful?
7. What makes a website easier or harder for your customers to use?
8. What changes would make your current process simpler?

## Simulated discovery notes (fictional)
**Property manager scenario:** Listing information sometimes changes after review. A manager would benefit from seeing whether a draft is awaiting review or needs changes.
**Local sponsor scenario:** A business owner wants to see a preview and correct advertising text before it becomes public.
These notes are fictional examples for requirements practice, not evidence of real interviews.

## Personas — working hypotheses
**Jordan, listing coordinator (fictional):** Wants accurate information and visible review status; may need to edit a draft after rejection.
**Casey, local shop owner (fictional):** Wants an easy way to submit sponsor details, preview the message, and see its approval status.
Both personas need validation with approved stakeholders.

## Approval flow
Draft → Submitted → In Review → Approved / Changes Requested → Published.
Only approved information is eligible for publication. A rejected item may be revised and resubmitted. No publication of unverified business claims.
